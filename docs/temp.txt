Insurance Enterprise Capability Map (ACORD-Aligned)
1. Product Development & Actuarial Management
 * Level 1: 1.0 Product Development & Actuarial Management
   * Level 2: 1.1 Market & Product Research
     * Level 3: 1.1.1 Competitive Market Analysis
       * Level 4: 1.1.1.1 Capture competitor rate filings, policy forms, and rule circulars (e.g., ISO/AAIS)
       * Level 4: 1.1.1.2 Benchmark market share, line-of-business loss ratios, and retention trends
       * Level 4: 1.1.1.3 Identify unserved/underserved risk segments and distribution coverage gaps
     * Level 3: 1.1.2 Product Ideation & Feasibility Assessment
       * Level 4: 1.1.2.1 Formulate target risk profiles, coverage definitions, and exclusions
       * Level 4: 1.1.2.2 Conduct preliminary legal, regulatory, and capital-requirement feasibility reviews
       * Level 4: 1.1.2.3 Estimate target loss costs, operational expense loads, and profit margins
   * Level 2: 1.2 Actuarial Pricing & Experience Analysis
     * Level 3: 1.2.1 Loss Cost Modeling & Data Aggregation
       * Level 4: 1.2.1.1 Extract, cleanse, and organize historical earned exposure and incurred loss data
       * Level 4: 1.2.1.2 Apply loss development factors (LDFs) to mature losses to ultimate values
       * Level 4: 1.2.1.3 Adjust exposure and loss trends for severity, frequency, and pure premium drift
     * Level 3: 1.2.2 Predictive Rate Modeling & Tariff Structuring
       * Level 4: 1.2.2.1 Develop Generalized Linear Models (GLMs) and machine-learning risk relativities
       * Level 4: 1.2.2.2 Calibrate rating tiers, minimum premiums, and territory/class code discount factors
       * Level 4: 1.2.2.3 Calculate fixed, variable, and unallocated loss adjustment expense (ULAE) loadings
     * Level 3: 1.2.3 Rate Indication & Disruption Analysis
       * Level 4: 1.2.3.1 Produce overall statewide/regional rate level indications by coverage
       * Level 4: 1.2.3.2 Perform in-force book disruption and policyholder premium impact profiling
       * Level 4: 1.2.3.3 Define rate capping, transition rules, and multi-year rollout schedules
   * Level 2: 1.3 Policy Form Design & Regulatory Filings
     * Level 3: 1.3.1 Contract Drafting & Endorsement Structuring
       * Level 4: 1.3.1.1 Draft core insuring agreements, conditions, deductibles, and definitions
       * Level 4: 1.3.1.2 Construct mandatory, optional, and state-specific endorsement schedules
       * Level 4: 1.3.1.3 Harmonize contract terms with primary, excess, and umbrella program requirements
     * Level 3: 1.3.2 Regulatory Filing Preparation & Management
       * Level 4: 1.3.2.1 Assemble filing packages via regulatory portals (e.g., SERFF)
       * Level 4: 1.3.2.2 Compile actuarial memorandums, rate exhibits, and explanatory rule manuals
       * Level 4: 1.3.2.3 Track Department of Insurance (DOI) objections, interrogatories, and approvals
   * Level 2: 1.4 Product Systems Configuration & Release
     * Level 3: 1.4.1 Rating Engine Configuration
       * Level 4: 1.4.1.1 Translate manual rate algorithms and tables into digital rating engines
       * Level 4: 1.4.1.2 Define cross-field rating dependencies, rounding rules, and tier validations
       * Level 4: 1.4.1.3 Execute regression test suites across multi-variable quoting scenarios
     * Level 3: 1.4.2 Document & Template Publishing
       * Level 4: 1.4.2.1 Configure dynamic variable tags and conditionality in document templates
       * Level 4: 1.4.2.2 Authorize digital signature layouts, notices, and billing schedule layouts
       * Level 4: 1.4.2.3 Deploy finalized product artifacts across Policy, Billing, and Claims engines
   * Level 2: 1.5 Actuarial Reserving & Capital Management
     * Level 3: 1.5.1 IBNR & Reserve Estimation
       * Level 4: 1.5.1.1 Execute Chain Ladder, Bornhuetter-Ferguson, and Cape Cod reserving models
       * Level 4: 1.5.1.2 Calculate Incurred But Not Reported (IBNR) and Incurred But Not Enough Reported (IBNER)
       * Level 4: 1.5.1.3 Evaluate Allocated Loss Adjustment Expense (ALAE) reserve sufficiency
     * Level 3: 1.5.2 Solvency & Capital Adequacy Modeling
       * Level 4: 1.5.2.1 Perform Risk-Based Capital (RBC) and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA) stress runs
       * Level 4: 1.5.2.2 Run Dynamic Financial Analysis (DFA) under extreme asset/liability stress regimes
       * Level 4: 1.5.2.3 Formulate Appointed Actuary Statements of Actuarial Opinion (SAO)

       * Level 4: 3.1.1.2 Extract text, asset tables, and entity names via OCR and document parsing
       * Level 4: 3.1.1.3 Standardize addresses, operations, and vehicle schedules against industry databases
     * Level 3: 3.1.2 Clearance & Conflict Management

       * Level 4: 3.1.2.2 Resolve broker-of-record conflicts and determine producer of record priority

   * Level 2: 3.2 Risk Assessment, Scoring & Qualification
     * Level 3: 3.2.1 External Data Enrichment & Verification
       * Level 4: 3.2.1.1 Order and parse MVRs, LexisNexis C.L.U.E., and commercial loss-run histories
       * Level 4: 3.2.1.2 Pull property geocoding, building characteristics, and wildland/flood zone data
       * Level 4: 3.2.1.3 Retrieve business financials, credit scores, OSHA violations, and D&B metrics
     * Level 3: 3.2.2 Appetite & Underwriting Rule Evaluation
       * Level 4: 3.2.2.1 Evaluate SIC/NAICS industry classifications against corporate underwriting appetite
       * Level 4: 3.2.2.2 Trigger automated straight-through processing (STP) rules for clean risk profiles
       * Level 4: 3.2.2.3 Flag hazard exposures, historical frequency anomalies, and moral risk attributes
     * Level 3: 3.2.3 Loss Control & Engineering Survey Management
       * Level 4: 3.2.3.1 Schedule on-site or virtual physical property and workplace safety inspections
       * Level 4: 3.2.3.2 Record risk engineer recommendations, COPE factors, and life-safety assessments
       * Level 4: 3.2.3.3 Track insured compliance with mandatory risk improvement recommendations
   * Level 2: 3.3 Pricing, Rating & Quote Structuring
     * Level 3: 3.3.1 Premium Calculation & Schedule Rating
       * Level 4: 3.3.1.1 Apply base rates, territory multipliers, and class code specific factors
       * Level 4: 3.3.1.2 Calculate schedule rating debits and credits within discretionary authority limits
       * Level 4: 3.3.1.3 Compute experience rating modifications (e.g., NCCI mod) and package discounts
     * Level 3: 3.3.2 Quote Generation & Negotiation
       * Level 4: 3.3.2.1 Assemble formal multi-option quote proposals with variable limits and deductibles
       * Level 4: 3.3.2.2 Specify subjectivities, warranty statements, and required conditional exclusions
       * Level 4: 3.3.2.3 Track underwriter negotiations, rate deviations, and expiring coverage benchmarks
   * Level 2: 3.4 Binding & Policy Issuance
     * Level 3: 3.4.1 Bind Order Processing & Verification
       * Level 4: 3.4.1.1 Validate satisfaction of all pre-bind underwriting subjectivities
       * Level 4: 3.4.1.2 Ingest signed bind requests, down payments, and mandatory legal disclosures
       * Level 4: 3.4.1.3 Confirm line assignment, capacity allotments, and aggregate exposure balances
     * Level 3: 3.4.2 Contract Construction & Document Delivery
       * Level 4: 3.4.2.1 Generate the policy declaration page, schedule of forms, and master policy terms
       * Level 4: 3.4.2.2 Assign unique statutory policy numbers and line-of-business identification tags
       * Level 4: 3.4.2.3 Deliver digital policy packages via secure channels to insureds, brokers, and lienholders
   * Level 2: 3.5 Mid-Term Lifecycle Management (Policy Servicing)
     * Level 3: 3.5.1 Policy Endorsements & Mid-Term Alterations
       * Level 4: 3.5.1.1 Process additions, modifications, and deletions to covered risks and locations
       * Level 4: 3.5.1.2 Calculate pro-rata and short-rate financial adjustments on coverage alterations
       * Level 4: 3.5.1.3 Issue certificates of insurance (COIs) and additional insured endorsements
     * Level 3: 3.5.2 Cancellations, Reinstatements & Non-Renewals
       * Level 4: 3.5.2.1 Issue statutory notices of cancellation for non-payment, fraud, or underwriting reasons
       * Level 4: 3.5.2.2 Manage formal rescission, conditional reinstatements, and lapse-in-coverage waivers
       * Level 4: 3.5.2.3 Prepare statutory non-renewal letters within jurisdiction-mandated day windows
     * Level 3: 3.5.3 Policy Renewal Processing
       * Level 4: 3.5.3.1 Execute automatic renewal sweeps and trigger pre-renewal risk re-evaluations
       * Level 4: 3.5.3.2 Generate renewal offers with re-rated payroll, updated sales receipts, and indexation
       * Level 4: 3.5.3.3 Track expiring-versus-renewal terms, quote follow-ups, and opt-out actions
   * Level 2: 3.6 Premium Audit Administration
     * Level 3: 3.6.1 Audit Assignment & Information Collection
       * Level 4: 3.6.1.1 Identify auditable policy structures (e.g., payroll, gross sales, units built)
       * Level 4: 3.6.1.2 Dispatch self-audit requests, hybrid mailings, or physical auditor orders

       * Level 4: 3.6.2.2 Calculate final earned exposure and compute additional or return audit premium
       * Level 4: 3.6.2.3 Process audit disputes, revisits, and non-compliance penalty endorsements

     * Level 3: 5.1.1 Intake & Initial Loss Recording
       * Level 4: 5.1.1.1 Capture incident time, loss location, loss narrative, and involved parties
       * Level 4: 5.1.1.2 Ingest initial third-party photos, dashcam footage, and police reports
       * Level 4: 5.1.1.3 Assign unique master claim identifiers and establish incident record shells
     * Level 3: 5.1.2 Policy Coverage Verification & Matching
       * Level 4: 5.1.2.1 Match loss occurrence date against policy active coverage terms and status
       * Level 4: 5.1.2.2 Verify covered perils, involved vehicles/properties, and named insured endorsements
       * Level 4: 5.1.2.3 Identify potential coverage defects, gaps, or breaches of contract warranty
     * Level 3: 5.1.3 Initial Coverage Position & Triage
       * Level 4: 5.1.3.1 Issue standard reservation of rights (ROR) letters or non-waiver agreements
       * Level 4: 5.1.3.2 Triage claim complexity, injury presence, and damage severity using rule engines
       * Level 4: 5.1.3.3 Route claims to specialized handling units (e.g., catastrophe, bodily injury, fast-track)
   * Level 2: 5.2 Special Investigation (SIU) & Fraud Detection
     * Level 3: 5.2.1 Predictive Fraud Scoring & Screening
       * Level 4: 5.2.1.1 Run incoming claim attributes against predictive fraud scorecards and network maps
       * Level 4: 5.2.1.2 Screen for staged accidents, prior loss frequency, and jump-in claimant anomalies
       * Level 4: 5.2.1.3 Analyze metadata, reverse-image databases, and digital photo alteration markers
     * Level 3: 5.2.2 Special Investigation Unit (SIU) Workflow
       * Level 4: 5.2.2.1 Refer claims meeting threshold indicators to SIU case management systems
       * Level 4: 5.2.2.2 Conduct Examinations Under Oath (EUO) and cross-examine social media footprints
       * Level 4: 5.2.2.3 Coordinate external fraud filings with state insurance fraud bureaus and regulators
   * Level 2: 5.3 Claims Investigation, Evaluation & Reserving
     * Level 3: 5.3.1 Liability & Incident Investigation
       * Level 4: 5.3.1.1 Interview insureds, adverse drivers, witnesses, and responding officers
       * Level 4: 5.3.1.2 Reconstruct accident scenes, compute stopping distances, and verify traffic controls

     * Level 3: 5.3.2 Physical Damage Appraisal & Inspection
       * Level 4: 5.3.2.1 Dispatch independent appraisers or manage self-service virtual photo estimating
       * Level 4: 5.3.2.2 Review itemized damage estimates, labor operations, and paint charges
       * Level 4: 5.3.2.3 Enforce OEM versus aftermarket parts usage and depreciation guidelines
     * Level 3: 5.3.3 Injury Evaluation & Medical Bill Review
       * Level 4: 5.3.3.1 Evaluate medical records, diagnostic scans, and treatment chronologies
       * Level 4: 5.3.3.2 Perform medical bill audits against state workers' comp/PIP fee schedules
       * Level 4: 5.3.3.3 Calculate pain and suffering, lost wages, and future medical treatment burdens
     * Level 3: 5.3.4 Loss Reserving Administration
       * Level 4: 5.3.4.1 Establish initial system-generated statistical reserves by line and coverage
       * Level 4: 5.3.4.2 Adjust adjuster individual case reserves based on emerging medical and repair facts
       * Level 4: 5.3.4.3 Maintain separate reserve categories for indemnity, ALAE, and medical expenses
   * Level 2: 5.4 Claims Settlement & Disbursement
     * Level 3: 5.4.1 Negotiation & Resolution Strategy
       * Level 4: 5.4.1.1 Establish settlement target ranges, walk-away numbers, and structured options
       * Level 4: 5.4.1.2 Conduct direct settlements with claimants or engage in formal ADR/mediation
       * Level 4: 5.4.1.3 Obtain signed general releases, covenants not to sue, and court minor approvals
     * Level 3: 5.4.2 Litigation Management & Legal Defense

       * Level 4: 5.4.2.2 Enforce outside counsel billing guidelines via legal e-billing audit engines
       * Level 4: 5.4.2.3 Review pleadings, motions for summary judgment, and trial exposure assessments
     * Level 3: 5.4.3 Payment Authorization & Indemnity Disbursement
       * Level 4: 5.4.3.1 Verify settlement amounts against individual adjuster authorization limits
       * Level 4: 5.4.3.2 Issue single, multi-party, and attorney lien release checks or real-time payments

   * Level 2: 5.5 Recovery (Subrogation, Salvage & Contribution)
